# YourListOnline

A streaming-focused to-do list at **yourlistonline.com.au**. Sign in with Twitch, add the tasks you're working through on stream, and drop a live overlay into OBS so your viewers can follow along. Accounts are shared with [BotOfTheSpecter](https://github.com/YourStreamingTools/BotOfTheSpecter) — one Twitch login covers both.

## What you can do

- **Manage your list** — add, edit, recategorise, complete, and remove tasks.
- **Organise by category** — keep streaming tasks separate from personal ones.
- **Show it on stream** — drop the overlay URL into a Browser Source in OBS, SLOBS, xSplit, or Wirecast.
- **Style the overlay** — font, colour (named or custom hex), font size, bold, shadow, bullet or numbered list.
- **Keep things private** — flag a task as Private so it stays off the overlay.
- **Hand off to your mods** — grant trusted Twitch moderators access to act as your channel and manage your list while you stream.
